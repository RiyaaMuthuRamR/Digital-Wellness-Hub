👶 Babies (0–2 Years)

Avoid entertainment screen time. Encourage talking, singing, tummy time, storytelling and face-to-face interaction with parents.

Screen Time

Avoid entertainment screens.

Sleep Goal

12–16 hours including naps.

Best Activities

Talking, music, sensory toys.

Healthy Screen Time Recommendations 📱

0–2 Years

🚫 Avoid

Focus on talking, eye contact and bonding.

2–5 Years

⏱ 1 Hour

Choose educational content with parents.

6–12 Years

⏱ 1–2 Hours

Take regular eye and movement breaks.

13–15 Years

📵 Balance

Limit bedtime scrolling and social media.

Healthy Sleep Routine 🌙

🌅 Morning

Sunlight, breakfast and active play.

📚 Afternoon

Learning time with outdoor breaks.

🍽 Evening

Family dinner without phones.

🌙 Bedtime

Storytime instead of screens for better sleep.

Best Offline Activities 🎨

👶 Babies

Music, peek-a-boo, colorful books and sensory toys.

🎨 Toddlers

Drawing, clay, puzzles, dancing and storytelling.

⚽ School Kids

Cycling, gardening, football, crafts and reading.

🌟 Teenagers

Photography, sports, painting, music, volunteering and journaling.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Activities

Cooking together, picnics, board games, yoga and nature walks.

Warning Signs Parents Should Notice 🚨

Tired Eyes

Frequent rubbing, headaches and eye strain.

Mood Changes

Anger or sadness when screen time ends.

Poor Sleep

Late-night scrolling or difficulty sleeping.

Less Outdoor Play

Losing interest in physical activities.

Less Family Interaction

Preferring screens over conversations.

Always Asking for Phone

Constantly demanding mobile devices.

Tips Every Parent Can Follow 💛

📵 Keep Bedrooms Screen-Free

Children sleep better without devices before bedtime.

🍽 Eat Meals Together

Phones stay away during family meals.

🌳 Outdoor Play Every Day

At least 20–60 minutes depending on age.

❤️ Be a Role Model

Children learn healthy habits from parents.
