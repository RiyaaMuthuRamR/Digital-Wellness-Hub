Bedtime Routine Checklist 🌟

Complete these before sleeping.

📵 Keep all phones away one hour before bedtime.
🛁 Take a warm bath before sleeping.
📚 Read a bedtime story together.
💧 Drink a glass of water.
🌙 Turn off bright lights.
❤️ Say Good Night and share gratitude.
